## Notes
- **Anchor offsets:** the sticky header (~78 px) is cleared with `scroll-padding-top: 88px` on `<html>`, which has the same effect as `scroll-margin-top` on every target. The spec accepts either.
- **Browser baseline:** the sideways-scroll fix uses `overflow: clip` (Safari 16+, Chrome 90+, Firefox 81+). On iOS 15 and older, the page can still be panned sideways at a few scroll positions.
